### Featured: product and AI life cycle, in five working projects

The projects below are working proof of how I approach AI. Each one starts from a business problem and keeps people in charge of decisions. Each one shows its controls openly. Together they cover the life cycle: *set the product strategy* → *decide which AI use cases to pursue* → *build with AI-native delivery* → *prove it works and is safe* → *govern delivery and prove the value*.

#### 1 · [Product Strategy Cockpit](https://github.com/geopap/product-strategy-cockpit): set the product strategy

**From company strategy to an outcome roadmap, with the evidence behind every bet: a driver tree, AI-assisted discovery, bets ranked by expected value, experiments with guardrails, and a quarterly strategy review.**

[**▶ Live demo**](https://geopap.github.io/product-strategy-cockpit/) · [Method](https://github.com/geopap/product-strategy-cockpit/blob/main/docs/METHOD.md)

[![Product Strategy Cockpit](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/geopap/product-strategy-cockpit/main/docs/screenshots/overview.png)](https://geopap.github.io/product-strategy-cockpit/)

- **Strategy you can calculate:** company goals → north star → driver tree, with a what-if simulator for subscription and partner revenue, retention and the north star.
- **Discovery with evidence:** AI groups customer feedback into themes and must cite it; a person confirms each theme before it can carry a bet.
- **Honest decisions:** product policy as code ("the first saving is always free"), guardrails that stop a commercial win that costs trust, and experiment readouts that catch broken tests.
- **A plan that adds up:** an outcome roadmap within capacity that shows how much of the target it really covers, and a signed quarterly review.

<sub>**Product lens:** strategy into roadmap, OKRs, discovery, freemium and monetisation, experimentation. **Technology lens:** capacity planning, dependencies on platform initiatives, hand-over to portfolio governance.</sub>

#### 2 · [AI Use-Case Intake](https://github.com/geopap/ai-use-case-intake): decide which AI use cases to pursue

**The front door for enterprise AI: one intake that decides what is worth doing, what the EU AI Act and GDPR require, and who has to approve it, before anyone builds or buys.**

[**▶ Live demo**](https://geopap.github.io/ai-use-case-intake/) · [Method](https://github.com/geopap/ai-use-case-intake/blob/main/docs/METHOD.md)

[![AI Use-Case Intake](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/geopap/ai-use-case-intake/main/docs/screenshots/register.png)](https://geopap.github.io/ai-use-case-intake/)

- **Decide what to do:** value, cost, payback and feasibility for every AI idea, with a clear *pursue now / invest to enable / quick experiment / park* call.
- **Explainable risk classification:** prohibited practices, Annex III high-risk areas, the Art. 6(3) exceptions and transparency duties, with the article cited for every result.
- **Proportionate governance:** a three-day fast track for minimal-risk tools, enhanced review for high-risk systems, and prohibited practices stopped at intake.

<sub>**Product lens:** discovery and prioritisation, business cases. **Technology lens:** demand management, build vs buy, vendor choice.</sub>

#### 3 · [Claude Code SDLC](https://github.com/geopap/claude-code-sdlc): build it with AI-native delivery

**An operating model for AI-native software delivery: 17 specialised agents, an 8-round pipeline and 8 mandatory quality gates, refined over 100+ production sprints.**

[**Pipeline**](https://github.com/geopap/claude-code-sdlc#the-pipeline) · [Quality gates](https://github.com/geopap/claude-code-sdlc#8-quality-gates) · [For engineering leaders](https://github.com/geopap/claude-code-sdlc#for-engineering-leaders)

[![Claude Code SDLC](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/geopap/claude-code-sdlc/main/docs/pipeline.png)](https://github.com/geopap/claude-code-sdlc)

- **Separation of duties:** agents that write code never approve it. Every change gets independent code, security and QA review.
- **Quality gates that cannot be skipped:** Definition of Ready before build, tests and UAT before done, a release checklist with rollback plans before close.
- **People stay accountable:** humans own scope, UAT sign-off, merges, database migrations and releases. The agents prepare; a person decides.

<sub>**Product lens:** discovery to release in one flow, with KPIs defined before build. **Technology lens:** SDLC design, code review, release management, cost-aware model routing.</sub>

#### 4 · [AI Eval Workbench](https://github.com/geopap/ai-eval-workbench): prove it works and is safe

**From AI failures to fixes, release decisions and audit-ready evidence. Evals that product teams use and AI governance can trust.**

[**▶ Live demo**](https://geopap.github.io/ai-eval-workbench/) · [Method](https://github.com/geopap/ai-eval-workbench/blob/main/docs/METHOD.md) · [Architecture](https://github.com/geopap/ai-eval-workbench/blob/main/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md)

[![AI Eval Workbench](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/geopap/ai-eval-workbench/main/docs/screenshots/failures.png)](https://geopap.github.io/ai-eval-workbench/)

- **Error analysis that leads somewhere:** blind human review, with notes grouped into failure modes, ranked by risk and sent to **Jira** with examples and a definition of done.
- **Judges you can trust:** each AI judge is calibrated against human labels before its scores count.
- **A real release decision:** a gate that recommends ship or no-ship, with must-never criteria, a named person's sign-off and an EU AI Act Art. 15 evidence pack.

<sub>**Product lens:** product-owned quality specs, release decisions. **Technology lens:** quality gates, regression testing, release management, tool-neutral integration.</sub>

#### 5 · [AI Portfolio Office](https://github.com/geopap/ai-portfolio-office): govern delivery and prove the value

**An AI-enabled Strategic Portfolio Office: value-driven portfolio governance, a human-in-the-loop PMO agent, and AI controls mapped to the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001 and NIST AI RMF.**

[**▶ Live demo**](https://geopap.github.io/ai-portfolio-office/) · [Operating model](https://github.com/geopap/ai-portfolio-office/blob/main/docs/FRAMEWORK.md) · [AI governance](https://github.com/geopap/ai-portfolio-office/blob/main/docs/AI-GOVERNANCE.md)

[![AI Portfolio Office](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/geopap/ai-portfolio-office/main/docs/screenshots/overview.png)](https://geopap.github.io/ai-portfolio-office/)

- **Value:** every initiative carries a P&L story, from approved business case to confirmed EBITDA, with clear **SCALE / CONTINUE / REVISE / STOP** calls.
- **AI where it removes work:** an agent runs the monthly collect → review → consolidate loop. It catches what people miss, such as a status reported green while finance actuals show a 19% overrun.
- **Governance by design:** a named human signs off before anything circulates, the audit trail is tamper-evident, and the AI only ever sees pseudonymised data.

<sub>**Product lens:** portfolio and roadmap governance, outcome KPIs. **Technology lens:** PMO and delivery operating model, budget control.</sub>

### How I work

- **Start from the business problem.** The technology comes second.
- **Teams own the how.** I set clear goals and priorities, then the team owns the outcome.
- **The agent prepares, a person decides.** AI should take on the repeatable work, never the accountability.
- **Make controls visible.** If a control can't be shown working, it isn't really in place.
- **Prove the value.** Every initiative needs a number it's measured against.
